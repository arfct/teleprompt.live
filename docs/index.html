Teleprompt

A teleprompter that listens. Read your script out loud and it scrolls with you, keeping the line you're on in the middle of the screen. Stop to think, and it waits.

Speech recognition runs on your iPhone, iPad or Mac, so what you say never leaves it. Type or paste a script, or open one from Google Docs.

Google Docs

Connecting a Google account is optional. If you do, Teleprompt asks for read-only access to your Google Docs and to the names of files in your Google Drive. It uses that only to list and search your Docs so you can pick one, and to load the text of the Doc you choose. It can't change, create or delete anything, and your data goes straight from Google to your device, never to Artifact. Details are in the privacy policy.

Requires iOS, iPadOS or macOS 26.
