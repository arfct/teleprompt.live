### Description

```
You're halfway down the page when you notice the text kept going without you. Now you're either rushing to catch up or squinting to find your place.

Teleprompt listens instead. Read your script out loud and it follows along word by word, keeping the line you're on in the middle of the screen. Stop to think, and it waits. Skip a sentence or say a word differently, and it keeps its place.

Words you've already said fade, so your eye goes to what's next. Small words like "the" and "of" are set lighter, so the ones you stress stand out.

• Follows your voice with speech recognition that runs on your device. What you say isn't recorded or sent anywhere.
• Scrolls at your pace and coasts to a stop when you pause, or moves line by line if you prefer.
• Type or paste a script, or open one from Google Docs. Docs are saved on the device, so they open offline.
• iPhone and iPad, portrait or landscape.

No account, no subscription, no ads. Google sign-in is only for opening Google Docs, and Teleprompt can only read them.

Requires iOS 26 or iPadOS 26.
```

## App Review information

- Sign-in required: **No** (the app works without any account).
- Notes:

```
Teleprompt is a teleprompter that follows the speaker's voice. To try it: open "Advice to Youth — Mark Twain" from the library, allow microphone and speech recognition access, and read the text aloud. The script scrolls to keep the current line centred and fades the words already spoken. Speech recognition runs on device.

Google sign-in is optional and only used to open the user's own Google Docs (read-only). This is a client for the user's Google account content, so Sign in with Apple isn't applicable (Guideline 4.8 exception). To test it: ⋯ → Sign In to Google, using the test account in the Sign-In Information fields, then + → Add Google Doc → "Teleprompt sample script". The Google project is in review with Google, so its consent screen shows an "unverified app" notice; tap Continue.
```

- Test account: the shared Google test account (arfct/internal `accounts.md`), entered in the Sign-In Information fields by a person.
